## [0.1.0] - Unreleased

### Added
- The addon loads for paladins on WoW Forever and says so with its version at login, in the
  client's language. Every other class loads nothing.
- A movable bar with one button per class in your group. Choose a blessing for each class with a
  right click; a left click blesses the next member of that class who lacks it, as a Greater
  Blessing when you know it and carry a Symbol of Kings. Each button shows how many lack it.
- An aura button (right click chooses the aura) and a Righteous Fury button that glows while
  Righteous Fury is missing.
- `/sbb` with `help`, `lock` and `reset`.
